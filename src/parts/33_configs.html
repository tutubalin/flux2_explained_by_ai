14.1 · The two guards in __init__

The constructor refuses to build a model whose numbers do not add up. Both checks are worth understanding rather than skimming:

◇ Beginner · why is theta only 2000?

Think of the 16 planes of one axis as the wheels of an odometer (§8.2). The fastest wheel turns once every 2π positions — it is what tells position 41 from position 42. The slowest wheel turns once every 2π·θ positions — it is what tells position 4 from position 4000, because the fast wheels have gone round and round and look identical again. Language models use θ = 10 000 or more because their positions run into the hundreds of thousands: they need a wheel slow enough not to wrap inside the context.

FLUX.2's coordinates are tiny by comparison — grid indices under ~200, text indices under 512, and a t axis that only ever reaches about 100 — so its slowest plane (period 2π·2000 ≈ 12 566 positions, FIG 15) is already far slower than the longest distance it will ever be asked about. inference A smaller base than 10 000 therefore costs nothing here and keeps the rest of the ladder in the range of distances the model actually needs to resolve; model.py simply states theta: int = 2000 (L19) without saying why.
