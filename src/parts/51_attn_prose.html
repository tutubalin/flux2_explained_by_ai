§12causal_attn_fn — the attention router · L758

Fifty-eight lines that decide, for every query in the sequence, which keys it is allowed to read. Despite its name there is nothing autoregressive here — the "causality" is of a different, more interesting kind.

⚠ Gotcha · the name lies

In an LLM, "causal attention" means token i may not see tokens after it — the triangular mask that makes text generation possible. causal_attn_fn contains no triangular mask at all: all three of its scaled_dot_product_attention calls pass is_causal=False. What it implements instead is a block-diagonal isolation rule: reference tokens are causally cut off from the rest of the sequence — they may read each other, and nobody's updates may leak into them. That single property is what makes the KV cache of §17 mathematically exact. Read the name as "references are sealed", not as "left-to-right".
