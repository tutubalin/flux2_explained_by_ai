FLUX.2, model.py,
every single line.

This is the 834-line file that defines a 32-billion-parameter image generator. It has no training code, no diffusion schedulers, no VAE — just the transformer that sits in the middle of FLUX.2 and predicts, at every step, which direction to push the image. Below, every class, every function and every line is explained from first principles, with hand-drawn diagrams showing exactly how the layers are wired and what shape the data has as it flows between them.

No prior knowledge of diffusion models is assumed. If you know what a matrix multiply does and have seen a bit of Python, you can follow this all the way to the end.
